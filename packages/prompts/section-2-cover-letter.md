# Section 2: Cover Letter Writing

router mode: `cover_letter`

Use this for drafting or customizing a cover letter for a specific role.

## Step 1: Analyze the Job Description

Extract: employer needs, required skills, key priorities.

## Step 2: Select Relevant Experience

Choose matching achievements, relevant projects, and supporting examples
from the candidate's background — never invent any.

## Step 3: Build the Letter Structure

1. **Opening** — target role and genuine interest.
2. **Value Proposition** — relevant experience and strengths.
3. **Evidence** — achievements connected to the employer's needs.
4. **Closing** — interest in discussing the opportunity, clear next step.

## Step 4: Customize for the Company

Add company-specific reasons for interest and role-specific value — no
generic filler that could apply to any employer.

## Step 5: Review Quality

Check: no generic templates or clichés, clear value proposition,
professional tone, strong alignment with the specific job description, no
unsupported claims.
