# Section 6.9: Professional Email and Message Writing

router mode: `task_6_9`

**Purpose:** improve workplace communication — recruiter emails, follow-ups, referral requests, thank-you notes, networking messages, manager communication, professional announcements.

1. Identify purpose: request, update, follow-up, introduction.
2. Identify the recipient: relationship, communication style, desired action.
3. Draft the message: greeting, context, main request/message, next step.
4. Improve clarity: remove unnecessary details and ambiguous language.
5. Final review: professionalism, tone, accuracy.
