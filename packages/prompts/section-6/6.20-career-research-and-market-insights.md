# Section 6.20: Career Research and Market Insights

router mode: `task_6_20`

**Purpose:** support informed career decisions.

1. Define the research goal: career question, industry, target role.
2. Gather relevant information: market trends, skill demand, role expectations.
3. Analyze opportunities: growth areas, competitive advantages, risks.
4. Translate insights into action: skills to build, roles to target, positioning changes.
5. Create a career strategy: clear recommendations, next steps, long-term direction.
