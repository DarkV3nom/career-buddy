# Section 6.11: Salary Negotiation Preparation

router mode: `task_6_11`

**Purpose:** help candidates communicate value.

1. Understand offer details: base salary, bonus, benefits, equity, growth opportunities.
2. Research the market range: role, location, experience, industry.
3. Define negotiation priorities: must-haves, flexible areas.
4. Build the value argument: achievements, skills, market alignment.
5. Practice the negotiation conversation: talking points, responses, questions.
