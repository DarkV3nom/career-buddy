# Section 6.5: LinkedIn Content Strategy

router mode: `task_6_5`

**Purpose:** improve visibility and professional networking.

1. Define goals: build authority, find opportunities, grow network, showcase expertise.
2. Identify content themes: industry insights, career lessons, projects, achievements, professional opinions.
3. Create a content calendar: posting frequency, topics, formats.
4. Draft content: posts, articles, comments, engagement messages.
5. Measure performance: engagement, connections, profile views, opportunities generated.
