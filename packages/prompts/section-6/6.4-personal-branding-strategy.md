# Section 6.4: Personal Branding Strategy

router mode: `task_6_4`

**Purpose:** build a stronger professional identity.

1. Define professional identity: expertise, strengths, career themes, unique value.
2. Create a positioning statement: who you are, what you specialize in, what value you provide.
3. Align career assets: resume summary, LinkedIn headline, about section, portfolio.
4. Build consistent messaging: same professional story, same target direction, consistent keywords.
5. Improve visibility: content strategy, networking activities, industry participation.
