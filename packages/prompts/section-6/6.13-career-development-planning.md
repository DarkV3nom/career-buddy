# Section 6.13: Career Development Planning

router mode: `task_6_13`

**Purpose:** create long-term growth plans.

1. Assess current position: skills, experience, strengths.
2. Define career goals: desired future roles, timeline, success measures.
3. Identify development areas: skill gaps, experience gaps.
4. Create a growth plan: learning goals, projects, mentorship, milestones.
5. Review progress: achievements, new skills, career movement.
