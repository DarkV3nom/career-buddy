# Section 6.7: Recruiter Communication Support

router mode: `task_6_7`

**Purpose:** improve interactions with recruiters.

1. Understand the situation: the recruiter's message, the candidate's objective, stage of process.
2. Determine the response goal: accept opportunity, request details, negotiate, follow up.
3. Draft the response: professional tone, clear message, appropriate length.
4. Review before sending: accuracy, tone, desired outcome.
