# Section 6.12: Offer Evaluation Support

router mode: `task_6_12`

**Purpose:** help compare job opportunities.

1. Collect offer information: compensation, benefits, role details.
2. Evaluate career fit: growth, learning, responsibilities, culture.
3. Compare options: pros and cons, weighted decision criteria.
4. Identify negotiation areas: compensation, title, flexibility, benefits.
5. Make a decision plan: recommendation framework, next actions.
