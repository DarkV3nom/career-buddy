# Section 6.6: Networking Strategy

router mode: `task_6_6`

**Purpose:** build meaningful professional connections.

1. Define networking goals: target companies, target professionals, desired outcomes.
2. Build a contact list: recruiters, hiring managers, industry professionals, alumni.
3. Create outreach messages: connection requests, introduction messages, follow-ups.
4. Build relationships: engage with content, ask relevant questions, share useful information.
5. Maintain the network: track conversations, follow-ups, relationship progress.
