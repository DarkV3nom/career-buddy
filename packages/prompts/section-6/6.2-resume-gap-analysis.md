# Section 6.2: Resume Gap Analysis

router mode: `task_6_2`

**Purpose:** identify weaknesses preventing better opportunities.

1. Review the current resume: structure, content, achievements, skills, career progression.
2. Compare against the target role: missing keywords, missing qualifications, weak positioning, irrelevant content.
3. Identify impact gaps: does it show ownership, business outcomes, leadership, measurable achievements?
4. Prioritize improvements: (1) critical ATS issues, (2) missing role requirements, (3) weak achievement statements, (4) formatting improvements.
5. Create an improvement plan with specific edits, missing content recommendations, and priority actions.
