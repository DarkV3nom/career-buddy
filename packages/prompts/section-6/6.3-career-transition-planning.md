# Section 6.3: Career Transition Planning

router mode: `task_6_3`

**Purpose:** help candidates move into a new industry or role.

1. Understand the current career: role, experience, skills, achievements, industry background.
2. Define the target career: desired roles, target industries, required qualifications.
3. Map transferable skills: relevant experience, leadership skills, technical skills, business capabilities.
4. Identify skill gaps by comparing current capabilities to target role requirements.
5. Create a transition roadmap: skills to learn, projects to complete, networking actions, resume changes.
