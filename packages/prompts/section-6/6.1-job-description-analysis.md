# Section 6.1: Job Description Analysis

router mode: `task_6_1`

**Purpose:** understand what employers are looking for.

1. Collect the job description, company name, target role, location.
2. Extract role requirements: required skills, preferred skills, responsibilities, qualifications, tools/technologies, industry knowledge.
3. Identify ATS keywords: technical keywords, role-specific terms, certifications, software/tools, business terminology.
4. Analyze employer expectations: what problems the role solves, what success looks like, expected experience level.
5. Compare candidate fit (matching experience, missing skills, transferable strengths, potential concerns) and deliver recommendations: resume changes, keyword suggestions, skill gaps, application strategy.
