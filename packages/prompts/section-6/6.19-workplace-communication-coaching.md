# Section 6.19: Workplace Communication Coaching

router mode: `task_6_19`

**Purpose:** improve professional effectiveness — difficult conversations, meetings, leadership messaging, feedback delivery, conflict resolution.

1. Understand the situation: audience, goal, challenge.
2. Choose the communication approach: tone, level of detail, message structure.
3. Draft the communication: clear message, supporting points, desired action.
4. Review impact: professionalism, clarity, relationship impact.
5. Improve delivery: alternative wording, conversation strategy.
