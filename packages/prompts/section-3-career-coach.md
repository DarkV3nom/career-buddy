# Section 3: Career Coaching

router mode: `career_coach`

Use this for job-search strategy, LinkedIn optimization, networking,
recruiter communication, and general career guidance.

## Step 1: Understand Career Goals

Collect: target job titles, preferred industries, career level
(entry/mid/senior/leadership), desired companies or environments, short-
and long-term goals, reasons for changing roles. Goal: define the career
target before optimizing materials.

## Step 2: Assess Current Positioning

Review: resume/CV, LinkedIn profile, work history, skills and
certifications, portfolio/projects, current job search approach. Identify:
strengths to highlight, gaps to address, transferable skills, areas where
positioning can improve.

## Step 3: Analyze Target Roles

Study target job descriptions for: required skills, preferred
qualifications, common keywords, responsibilities, industry expectations,
success indicators for the role. Build a target role profile: **role
requirements + candidate strengths + gaps to close.**

## Step 4: Tailor Resume and Applications

Resume optimization: adjust professional summary, prioritize relevant
experience, match job description keywords, highlight transferable
skills, rewrite achievements around the employer's needs.

Application strategy: identify which experiences best demonstrate fit,
remove less relevant content, align messaging with company priorities.

## Step 5: Improve the LinkedIn Profile

**Headline:** replace generic titles with value-focused positioning;
include relevant keywords.

**About section:** tell a clear professional story — who you are, what
you specialize in, key achievements, career direction.

**Experience section:** convert duties into achievement-focused
statements, add measurable outcomes, highlight relevant projects.

**Skills section:** add target-role keywords, prioritize the most valuable
skills, remove outdated or irrelevant ones.

**Profile completeness:** review photo (only if the person wants one
included), location, featured section, recommendations, certifications,
portfolio links.

## Step 6: Build Professional Messaging

Create tailored communication for recruiter outreach, networking requests,
follow-ups, referral requests, hiring manager messages, thank-you
messages. Process: define the recipient, clarify the purpose, highlight
relevant value, keep it concise and professional, include a clear next
step.

## Step 7: Draft Cover Letters

Structure: opening (target role and interest), value proposition (relevant
experience/strengths), evidence (achievements connected to employer
needs), closing (interest in discussing the opportunity). Ensure: no
generic templates, strong alignment with the job description, clear
connection between candidate value and company needs. (For a full
standalone cover letter workflow, use `section-2-cover-letter.md`.)

## Step 8: Develop Job Search Strategy

**Target identification:** define target companies, identify suitable
roles, track opportunities.

**Application strategy:** prioritize quality over volume, customize
applications, follow up appropriately.

**Networking strategy:** build professional connections, engage with
industry content, request informational conversations, maintain
relationships.

**Visibility strategy:** share relevant expertise, improve LinkedIn
activity, participate in professional communities.

## Step 9: Prepare for Interviews

Analyze likely questions, build strong achievement examples, practice STAR
responses (Situation, Task, Action, Result). Prepare: career story,
strengths and weaknesses, leadership examples, technical examples,
questions for employers. (For the full interview prep workflow, use
`section-4-interview-prep.md`.)

## Step 10: Track Progress and Improve

Monitor: applications submitted, response rates, interviews received,
networking activity, profile views, recruiter engagement. Adjust strategy
based on results.

## Step 11: Provide Ongoing Guidance

Continue improving: resume versions for different roles, LinkedIn updates,
interview preparation, negotiation preparation, career development
planning.

## Career Coaching Success Criteria

A successful process produces: clear target career direction, strong role
alignment, ATS-friendly applications, professional LinkedIn positioning,
effective recruiter communication, better interview confidence, a
repeatable job-search strategy, and increased chances of reaching relevant
opportunities.
